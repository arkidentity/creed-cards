## Scrutiny pass — 2026-09-06

**Test applied to every card:** can it carry a real *definition*, a real
*scripture*, AND a real *historical context* (a council, a heresy, a theologian,
a documented development)? Definition and scripture are never the problem.
**Historical context is the filter** — plus a second filter: can ARK write it
*irenically*, without picking a side in a live intramural fight? ARK deliberately
kept Election out of Deck 1 for exactly that reason.

Verdict codes: ✅ solid on all three · ⚠️ thin history (needs a sharper hook or a cut) · 🔥 theologically loaded (hard to be neutral) · ♻️ redundant with another card.

### Candidate replacements (6) — all with checkable history

1. **The Canon of Scripture** — Marcion (~144), the Muratorian Fragment (~180), Athanasius' 367 list, Carthage (397), Trent (1546). Fills a real gap: Deck 1 has Inspiration and Authority but nothing on *how we got the 66 books*.
2. **The Clarity & Sufficiency of Scripture** (perspicuity) — Reformation vs Rome's magisterium; "Scripture interprets Scripture."
3. **The Perseverance of the Saints — the views** — Synod of Dort (1618) vs the Remonstrants; Heb 6 / John 10:28. *(Or fold into Assurance.)*
4. **Common Grace** — Kuyper's *De gemeene gratie* (1902); the 1924 CRC "three points" and the Protestant Reformed split. Why unbelievers do good and culture has worth.
5. **The Filioque & the Great Schism** — added at Toledo (589); formal rupture (1054). Currently only a footnote under the Nicene Creed.
6. **Sanctification — the traditions** (Wesleyan / Reformed / Keswick / Lutheran) — complements Deck 1's Sanctification card; well-documented history.

### Category-structure findings

1. **The `salvation` slug is overloaded** — "Covenant & Redemptive History" (8) + "Deeper Soteriology" (7) = 15 cards under one browse colour, while Pneumatology has 4. **Recommend splitting off a real `covenant` category** (Covenant, The Law and Its Purpose, Typology, Already/Not Yet, Election) so the app's category browse and quiz tagging stay balanced.
2. **Deck 2 has no Scripture category** — Deck 1's "Holy Scripture" is only 3 cards. Adding **The Canon** + **Clarity & Sufficiency** (and moving **The Spirit and the Word** here) gives Deck 2 a small `scripture` group and closes a genuine doctrinal gap.
3. **`creeds` as a 9th taxonomy slug** unique to Deck 2 — keep it; it's the deck's signature. With the Filioque card it's 10.
